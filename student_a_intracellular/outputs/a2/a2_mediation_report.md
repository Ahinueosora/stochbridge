## Flow tested (gbmMINER Fig 5B–D logic)

```
CRP knockout or activating-region deletion  ──►  CRP activity (Crp-1 iModulon)  ──►  target gene
                 └───────────── direct path? (should vanish if mediated) ─────────────┘
```

An edge is `causal: true` when three things hold:
- the perturbation changes the target (BH FDR < 0.05);
- the knockout and the domain deletions change it in the same direction;
- the change is abolished once CRP activity is conditioned on. "Abolished" means the conditioned p-value is above 0.05, the mediator is significant (p < 0.05), and at least 50% of the effect is explained by CRP activity.

The rule was fixed before the results were seen.

## Samples (all from the `crp` project, so every comparison is batch-matched)

| Group | Conditions | n |
|---|---|---|
| CRP knockout (`del_crp`) | fructose, glucose, glycerol | 9 |
| Domain deletion (`crp_delAr1`, `crp_delAr2`, `crp_delAr1delAr2`) | glycerol | 8 |
| Wild type | fructose (1), glycerol (2) | 3 |

Carbon source is a covariate. There is no glucose wild type, so the glucose knockouts only contribute to within-carbon variation.

## Why the mediator is CRP activity rather than CRP mRNA

The brief says to condition on "CRP's own measured expression level". That fails here:
- **Knockouts alone:** knockout status and CRP mRNA correlate at r = −0.998, so conditioning erases every effect through collinearity alone.
- **Adding the domain deletions** breaks the collinearity (R² = 0.18). But those strains make normal CRP mRNA (log TPM 10–11) and lose CRP *function*, so mRNA is not the quantity that carries the effect.

Both mediators were run:
- **CRP mRNA:** 0 edges pass. The mRNA term is significant for 68 targets but never abolishes the perturbation effect. That is the correct answer to "does CRP mRNA level mediate this?".
- **CRP activity:** edges do pass. It is computed per target with that gene left out of the iModulon projection; with no gene dropped this reproduces A.csv at r = 0.9997.

Only 3 of the 21 causal targets are Crp-1 iModulon members, so the result is not circular.

Most edges are classic CRP-activated operons: maltose (malK, lamB), ribose (rbsABCR), the flagellar master regulator (flhDC), uhpT, galK and araJ.

**4 edges (fecE, mhpF, nadC, gyrA) move opposite to their RegulonDB "+" annotation.** The data say they depend on CRP activity, but the direction points to an indirect route, such as growth effects. Treat them as causal but not as direct activation.

### Lac operon

- lacZ: total effect -0.83, FDR 0.30 → no perturbation effect
- lacY: total effect -0.21, FDR 0.61 → no perturbation effect
- lacA: total effect -0.02, FDR 0.94 → no perturbation effect
- lacI: total effect -0.03, FDR 0.94 → no perturbation effect

No lac edge can be validated:
- **No inducer in the test samples.** None of the `crp` samples contain an inducer, so LacI keeps the operon off in wild type and knockout alike.
- **No inducer anywhere in PRECISE-1K.** There is no lactose, IPTG or TMG sample. This is confirmed from the metadata and from lacZ expression: its maximum log2 TPM is 6.7, while the top 1% of genes sit at ≥ 12.
- **The induced samples elsewhere can't test causation.** The K-12 modulome has 20 samples with an induced operon (project `srna_oe`, 1 mM IPTG, lacZ ≈ 11.5–12). They are a single saturating dose in lacI^q/plasmid strains with no CRP perturbation. They can inform the induced expression level for A3, but cannot validate CRP → lac causally.

## Error rates of the rule (simulation, `tests/test_mediation.py`)

With 18 samples and a clear effect:
- a truly mediated target is missed about 4–5% of the time (the α of the "abolished" test);
- a target whose effect bypasses the mediator is wrongly flagged 0.1% of the time.

The rule is conservative: it errs toward `causal: false`.

## Limitations

- **Very few controls.** There are only 3 wild-type samples, and the domain deletions exist only in glycerol. Power is low, so an edge that fails the test despite a real effect ("not explained by mediator") is not evidence of no mediation.
- **The activity readout reflects all of CRP's targets.** Crp-1 activity is estimated from CRP's other targets, so anything that moves all of them together, beyond carbon source, would look like mediation. cAMP is only partly controlled through the carbon covariate.
- **The Crp-2 iModulon was not used as a mediator.** Its activity is nearly collinear with perturbation (r = −0.95).
- **This is a causal-mediation / conditioning test, not Mendelian randomization**, as the brief requires.

## Consequence for A3

A3 gets 21 `causal: true` CRP edges, none of them in the lac operon. The Ozbudak lac circuit takes its CRP → lac and LacI → lac edges from RegulonDB and the literature, marked as prior-knowledge edges, not A2-validated ones.
